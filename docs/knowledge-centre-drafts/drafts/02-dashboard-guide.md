# Dashboard Guide

**Audience:** USER  
**Category:** Dashboard Guide  
**Module:** Dashboard  
**Summary:** What you see on the main Dashboard after login: compliance score, score trend, properties at a glance, and what to do next.

---

## Purpose

The Dashboard is your home page in the client portal. It shows your overall compliance score, how it has changed over time, a list of your properties with their status (e.g. Green, Amber, Red), and—for new users—optionally a setup checklist. This guide explains each part and where to go for more detail or action.

---

## When to use this guide

- You want to understand what the numbers and badges on the Dashboard mean.
- You are not sure what “Score trend” or “Portfolio vs Property” means.
- You want to know where to click to fix a property showing Amber or Red.

---

## Steps

1. **Open the Dashboard** — After login you usually land here. If not, click **Dashboard** in the sidebar.
2. **Check your compliance score** — The score (0–100) and grade (e.g. A, B, C) show how healthy your portfolio is. Green/low risk is good; Red or “action required” means you have overdue or missing items.
3. **Use the score trend** — The **Score Trend (90 days)** card lets you switch between **Portfolio** (all properties) and **Property** (one property) to see how the score changed over time. Use this to see if things are improving after you upload evidence.
4. **Review your properties** — Each property is shown with a status: **Green** (valid), **Amber** (attention needed), **Red** (overdue). Click a property to open its detail page and see requirements and documents.
5. **Complete the setup checklist** (if shown) — New users may see a short checklist (e.g. add property, upload evidence). You can complete items or skip; the checklist may disappear once done or dismissed.
6. **Use the links** — From the Dashboard you can go to **Compliance** (requirements), **Documents** (upload evidence), **Compliance Score** (full score and drivers—via the Compliance Score card or link on this page; it is not in the main sidebar), and **Help Centre** (in the footer).

---

## What happens next

- Clicking a property takes you to the **Property detail** page where you can manage requirements and evidence for that property.
- The score and trend update after the system recalculates (scheduled); there may be a short delay after you upload evidence or change dates.
- If your plan includes **Operations** (e.g. Work orders, Risk signals), you may see related cards or links on the Dashboard; otherwise those menu items are hidden.

---

## Common mistakes / troubleshooting

- **Score seems wrong:** The score is recalculated on a schedule. Wait a few minutes after uploading evidence or changing dates, or check the **Compliance Score** page for “drivers” that explain what is pulling the score down.
- **No properties shown:** Add your first property via **Properties → Add Property**.
- **Setup checklist keeps appearing:** Depends on product behaviour; you may be able to dismiss or complete it. If it blocks you, contact support.
