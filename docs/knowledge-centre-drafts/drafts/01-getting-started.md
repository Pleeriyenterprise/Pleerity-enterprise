# Getting Started

**Audience:** USER  
**Category:** Getting Started  
**Module:** Dashboard  
**Summary:** First steps after you log in: where to go, what to do first, and how to stay on top of compliance.

---

## Purpose

This guide helps new users understand the main areas of the client portal and the recommended order of actions: add a property, manage compliance requirements, and upload evidence so your compliance score and reminders work correctly.

---

## When to use this guide

- You have just logged in for the first time (or set your password after onboarding).
- You are not sure what to do after login.
- You want a short overview of the main menu and where to find key tasks.

---

## Steps

1. **Log in** at the client portal (e.g. **Login → Client**). Use the link or URL your organisation gave you.
2. **Check your Dashboard** (first page after login). You will see your compliance score (if you have properties and data) and a list of properties. If you see a setup checklist, you can follow it or skip and go to the next step.
3. **Add a property** if you have none: open **Properties** in the sidebar → **Add Property** → enter address and details → save. Your plan may limit how many properties you can add.
4. **Open Compliance** (sidebar) to see requirements and due dates. You can group by property or by requirement type. Fix overdue or expiring items first.
5. **Upload evidence** (e.g. Gas Safety Certificate): open **Documents** → choose property and requirement → select file → upload. After the system analyses the document, confirm or edit the expiry and issue dates in the **Confirm details** step.
6. **Set notification preferences** if you want reminders: **Settings → Notifications** → turn on **Daily Reminders** (and enable **Expiry Reminders** and optionally SMS after verifying your phone).

---

## What happens next

- Your **compliance score** (on the Dashboard and on the **Compliance Score** page—reached via the Dashboard card or link to `/compliance-score`) will update after the system recalculates (this can take a short time after you upload evidence or change dates).
- **Reminders** are sent automatically once per day if you have items due and reminders are enabled in Settings.
- Use **Help Centre** (in the footer at the bottom of the page) to search for more detailed guides (e.g. “How to Add a Property”, “Uploading Evidence”).

---

## Common mistakes / troubleshooting

- **“Client not found” or blank dashboard:** You may have logged in with the wrong portal (e.g. Admin). Use the Client login link.
- **Cannot add property:** Your account may not be fully set up yet (provisioning), or you may have reached your plan’s property limit. Contact support or check **Settings → Billing** for your plan.
- **Reminders not arriving:** Ensure **Settings → Notifications** has **Daily Reminders** and **Expiry Reminders** turned on, and that you have at least one requirement due in the reminder window.
