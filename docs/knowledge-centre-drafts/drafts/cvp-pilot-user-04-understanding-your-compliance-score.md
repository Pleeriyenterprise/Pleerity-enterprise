Title: Understanding your compliance score  
Audience: USER  
Category: Compliance Score  
Status: draft  
Version: 1.0  

Summary: Your compliance score is guidance based on your properties and requirements—not a guarantee of legal compliance.  

Purpose: Help you interpret the score, drivers, and any pending states without over-interpreting the colour or number.  

Expected outcome: You know what improves the score, what does not, and when to wait for the system to finish updating.  

## Before you start

1. You should have at least one **property** and visible **requirements**.  
2. Open **Settings → Billing** if you are unsure whether **PDF report export** or other score-related exports are on your plan (`reports_pdf` is **Portfolio+** in the product feature matrix; **Solo** does not include PDF report export).  

## Steps

1. **Click** **Compliance score** in the sidebar or open `/compliance-score`.  
   - **Purpose:** View the detailed score page.  
   - **Expected result:** Headline score, grade or colour, explanatory text, and optional drivers or recommendations.  

2. **Read** the headline **score** and any **message** about freshness or recalculation.  
   - **Purpose:** Understand whether the number is mid-update.  
   - **Expected result:** Plain-language guidance; if pending appears, treat it as normal shortly after uploads or admin actions.  

3. **Open** drivers or what affects my score sections if your UI provides them.  
   - **Purpose:** Tie the number to specific requirements or properties.  
   - **Expected result:** A short list of items you can act on (upload evidence, renew a certificate, confirm dates).  

4. **Click** through to **Requirements** or **Documents** from any provided links on items you want to fix.  
   - **Purpose:** Move from insight to action.  
   - **Expected result:** The relevant list or detail page opens.  

5. **Optional — export:** If your plan includes report export, use export controls on this page or under **Reports** (`/reports`) exactly as your UI shows.  
   - **Purpose:** Download summaries based on available records.  
   - **Expected result:** A file download or a message if your plan does not include the export.  

## What you should see

- A single headline portfolio score on the compliance score page, based on your current property records and requirements.
- Numbers that align with **Requirements** after processing catches up.

## Common issues

- **Issue:** Score dropped after I uploaded a certificate.  
  **What it means:** Dates might need confirmation, the obligation might still be in review, or another property worsened—not necessarily a broken upload.  
  **What to do:** Open the document detail, check statuses, then refresh the score page after a few minutes.  

- **Issue:** Score looks high but I still have red rows in Requirements.  
  **What it means:** The score reflects your portfolio as a whole; individual obligations can still need attention.  
  **What to do:** Work the **Requirements** list, not only the headline number.  

## Important notes

- The score is **operational guidance**, not legal advice and not a regulator sign-off.  
- The platform may take a short time to refresh after uploads or admin actions — check requirement and document status, then refresh the score page.  
- **Professional** features (for example some analytics blocks on Reports) may appear only on higher tiers—check Billing.  

## When to contact support / escalate

- The score page shows a hard error.  
- A driver points to a requirement that does not exist anywhere in the portal.  
- The headline score disagrees with **Requirements** after a long wait and a refresh.
