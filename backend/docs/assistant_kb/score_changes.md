# Why a compliance score goes up or down

The compliance score can change when the data in the portal changes. Below are typical reasons for **drops** and **increases**. Use the score drivers and requirement lists in the portal context to give the user a concrete explanation for *their* score change when that data is available.

## Typical reasons for a score **drop**

- **New overdue items**  
  A requirement passed its due or expiry date without valid, accepted evidence (for example a certificate expired or a check was not renewed).

- **More items expiring soon**  
  Certificates or checks are now within the "expiring soon" window until they are renewed.

- **Fewer satisfied requirements**  
  A requirement that was in good standing is no longer satisfied—for example evidence was removed, or an expiry date passed so the accepted evidence no longer counts.

- **Document removed or not yet accepted**  
  A document that was supporting a requirement was deleted, rejected, or is still awaiting review.

- **New requirement added**  
  A new requirement (for example for a new property or regulation) starts as not satisfied, which can lower the property or portfolio score until evidence is provided and accepted.

## Typical reasons for a score **increase**

- **Overdue items resolved**  
  Valid evidence was added and accepted for previously overdue requirements (for example a new certificate uploaded and accepted).

- **Documents uploaded and accepted**  
  New or replacement documents were uploaded and accepted after review, improving requirement status.

- **Certificates renewed**  
  Expiring or expired items were renewed (new expiry date in the future).

- **Maintenance or actions cleared**  
  Open maintenance issues or linked actions were resolved where they were affecting the score.

- **Fewer items expiring soon**  
  Items that were "expiring soon" were renewed or are no longer in scope.

When the user asks "why did my score drop?" or "why did my score go up?", use score drivers, requirement statuses, and counts (overdue, expiring soon, valid) to explain in plain language. Do not quote point changes or internal weighting.
